    How Spaced Repetition & Mastery Work

    • No 1-Shot Mastery: Answering a question correctly once never marks it as "Mastered". It enters the Reviewing stage with an initial 1-day interval.
    • Mastery Rule: A question reaches Mastered only after $\ge 3$ consecutive unassisted objective successes across at least 2 separate review intervals (or $\ge 80\%$ lifetime independent accuracy with $\ge 3$ independent correct answers).
    • Hints Tracked Separately: Viewing a hint before answering is recorded as an assisted attempt (`hintsUsed`). Even if you submit the right answer afterward, it does not advance independent mastery and schedules a short 12-hour follow-up review.
    • Misses Return Sooner: An incorrect answer resets the card's consecutive streak to 0, marks its status as Needs Work, and schedules immediate review (within 10 minutes).
    • Flashcards vs. Objective Grading: Flashcard self-ratings (Again, Hard, Good, Easy) schedule spaced review intervals (`10m`, `1d`, `3d`, `6d+`) but are tracked in a separate `selfRated` counter so self-reported recall is never conflated with objectively graded accuracy.
